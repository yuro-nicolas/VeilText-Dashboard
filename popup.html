How It Works

VeilText scans page text with a multi-pattern matching engine you choose in Settings:

Aho-Corasick builds a trie of your keywords plus failure links, forming a finite-state automaton that scans text in a single deterministic pass. O(n + m + z) time.

Wu-Manber instead precomputes SHIFT and HASH tables from short blocks of your keywords, letting the scanner skip ahead through text and only verify matches when a skip reaches zero.

Privacy

All filtering is done entirely on your device. No page content, keywords, or user data is ever sent to any server.
